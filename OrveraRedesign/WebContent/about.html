OUR STORY

Sustainability Meets Sophistication

Where farm waste becomes the future of premium B2B packaging.

Crafting Change to Protect Our Planet

At Orvera, we don't just create packaging — we create change that protects our planet. Founded with a belief that India's biggest environmental problem has an Indian solution, Orvera has emerged as the country's first sustainable packaging brand built entirely from agricultural waste.

Our journey began with one simple question: "Why are we still using plastic when India's farms produce millions of tonnes of rice husk and sugarcane bagasse every year—materials that decompose naturally, cost almost nothing, and have the potential to replace conventional plastic packaging in many applications?"

That question inspired us to think differently about packaging. Instead of viewing agricultural waste as a disposal problem, we saw it as an opportunity to create products that are environmentally responsible, commercially viable, and built for the future. Our goal is to make sustainable packaging practical, affordable, and accessible for businesses across India.

As a B2B brand, we work directly with businesses — cafes, restaurants, corporate offices, event organizers, and hotels — supplying eco-friendly packaging products that are plastic-ban compliant, biodegradable, and proudly made from India's own farm waste.

We understand that switching from plastic isn't just a business decision — it's a statement. That's why every Orvera product is designed to be as functional as it is sustainable, so businesses never have to choose between quality and conscience.

What sets us apart is our commitment to building an entirely Indian solution — local raw materials, local supply chain, and local impact. When you choose Orvera, you're not just switching packaging. You're supporting India's farmers, reducing landfill waste, and taking a stand against the plastic crisis.

Beyond products, we are building a movement — a community of businesses and individuals who believe that sustainability isn't a premium feature. It's the only way forward. Our promise remains the same — to make eco-friendly packaging the default choice for every business in India. Not someday. Today.

Welcome to Orvera — where farm waste becomes the future.
