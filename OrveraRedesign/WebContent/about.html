ABOUT US

Our Mission & Vision

Combining design elegance with absolute sustainability to eliminate plastic waste in premium hospitality.

Crafting a Greener Tomorrow

At ORVERA, we believe that modern businesses shouldn't have to choose between aesthetic luxury and ecological responsibility. Founded in 2026, we set out to redesign dining essentials—cutlery, bottles, and food packaging—using organic, biodegradable, and circular materials that look and feel premium in every setting.

Every year, billions of single-use plastic utensils end up in landfills and oceans. By upgrading to ORVERA's FSC-certified forest woods, bamboo, and bio-composites, your brand actively participates in restoring natural balance, satisfying corporate ESG targets while elevating customer dining experiences.

OUR VALUES

How We Define Circular Design

100% Traceability

We trace every piece of timber and bamboo back to its sustainable forest origin, ensuring no deforestation occurs.

Zero Toxins

Our manufacturing uses absolute zero chemical coatings, synthetic glosses, or plastics, remaining completely food-safe.

Premium Durability

Through high-pressure fiber engineering, our wooden sets are splinter-free and heat-resistant up to 120°C.

Soil-to-Soil Cycle

Once discarded, our products degrade completely back into organic compost within 90 days, feeding new plant life.
