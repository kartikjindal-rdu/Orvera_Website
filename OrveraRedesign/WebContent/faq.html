Frequently Asked Questions

Clear details on material sustainability, wholesale quotes, branding, and shipping processes.

Are your cutlery sets reuseable or single-use?

+

Our classic wooden cutlery is heavy-duty and can be reused multiple times for dry foods or washed gently, but is certified 100% compostable, making it ideal for events looking to eliminate single-use plastics.

What is the lead time for custom brand engravings?

+

Custom laser-branded engravings typically take 10-14 business days for manufacturing and approval before shipping. Bulk wholesale shipments without engraving ship within 3-5 business days.

Are your wood products sourced sustainably?

+

Yes, all ORVERA wood and bamboo tablewares are FSC-certified (Forest Stewardship Council), meaning they are harvested from responsibly managed forests that provide environmental, social, and economic benefits.

Can you design custom packaging dimensions?

+

Yes! We offer fully custom dimensions, paper weights, and shapes for our premium packaging. Our design team works with your product blueprints to engineer the perfect custom fit. Contact our sales team at orverabottles@gmail.com to discuss custom packaging specifications.

What is ORVERA's core material composite?

+

ORVERA products are built from organic plant fibers, including bamboo pulp, sugarcane bagasse, and wheat straw, bound with natural starches. They are 100% bio-based and leave zero microplastics in the soil.

Can the Eco Bottles hold hot liquids?

+

Our current line of bottles is optimized for cold and ambient liquids up to 50°C (122°F). We are actively testing high-temperature plant-wax coatings for coffee and tea applications, planned for release in late 2026.

How long does decomposition take under home conditions?

+

In standard home composting conditions, ORVERA products break down within 45 to 120 days depending on soil moisture, temperature, and microbial activity. In industrial facilities, it takes less than 30 days.

Are Orvera products microwave and freezer safe?

+

Yes. Our Eco Cutlery and fiber-based packaging plates/bowls are microwave-safe for reheating up to 2 minutes, and they are also freezer-safe. They retain their structural integrity under moderate heating.

How can businesses start a B2B volume partnership?

+

Businesses can reach out directly via our Contact page or by emailing us at orverabottles@gmail.com. Our corporate accounts team manages volume pricing, annual supply agreements, and custom brand embossing.
