The Sustainability Dilemma

Why traditional packaging fails our planet, and how ORVERA's natural design offers a circular path forward.

⚠️ The Plastic Crisis

  • 🛑

    400 Million Tons / Year

    Global plastic production exceeds 400 million tons annually, with single-use packaging being the largest contributor.

  • ☠️

    Universal Contamination

    Microplastics have been found in human blood, rainwater, and remote ecosystems, surviving for over 450 years.

  • 🏭

    Heavy Carbon Footprint

    Manufacturing a single conventional PET plastic bottle releases approximately 85 grams of CO₂ into our atmosphere.

🌱 The ORVERA Cure

  • 🌾

    100% Agri-Waste Composite

    We repurpose local crop waste (rice husk, sugarcane bagasse) into a durable structural shell, creating a zero-wood circular economy.

  • ⏳

    12-Week Natural Decay

    Our composite fully decomposes in home or backyard compost within 80-90 days, leaving absolutely zero synthetic residues behind.

  • 📉

    90% Lower Emissions

    ORVERA bottles release just 8.2g of carbon per unit, running on bio-energy and reducing production emissions drastically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about our technology and release timeline.

ORVERA bottles are designed primarily as single-use biodegradable alternatives to plastic beverage bottles. However, they can be refilled and reused multiple times for water or dry ingredients before composting.
The external fiber shell is treated with a natural, plant-based water-repellent coating. This prevents the outer shell from absorbing condensation, humidity, or splashes, keeping the bottle sturdy during use.
You can simply toss it into your household food waste bin, a backyard compost bin, or an industrial composting stream. Because it is 100% bio-based, it degrades safely without toxic residues.
Our current lining supports still water, carbonated soft drinks, juices, and low-alcohol beverages (up to 15% ABV). We are currently testing hot beverage liners for tea and coffee.
